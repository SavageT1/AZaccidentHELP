Car Accident Help in Arizona

If you were rear-ended, T-boned, sideswiped, or hit by an uninsured driver, consider documenting the scene and obtaining appropriate medical care. This page provides general information, not legal advice.

What to document after a crash

  • Photos of vehicle damage, skid marks, road conditions, and traffic signals
  • Police report number, officer name, and witness contact information
  • Emergency room notes, follow-up visits, and prescriptions
  • Lost work time, rideshare costs, and repair estimates

Common car accident questions

How fast should I act? As soon as possible, because evidence and witness details can disappear quickly.

What if the other driver was uninsured? Insurance coverage varies. A licensed attorney can discuss how available policies may apply.

Should I speak with the insurance company? You may wish to consult a licensed attorney before making decisions about a potential claim.
