Car Accident Help in Arizona

If you were rear-ended, T-boned, sideswiped, or hit by an uninsured driver, the first step is simple: protect the evidence and get medical documentation right away.

What to document after a crash

  • Photos of vehicle damage, skid marks, road conditions, and traffic signals
  • Police report number, officer name, and witness contact information
  • Emergency room notes, follow-up visits, and prescriptions
  • Lost work time, rideshare costs, and repair estimates

Common car accident questions

How fast should I act? As soon as possible, because evidence and witness details can disappear quickly.

Can I still get help if the other driver was uninsured? Yes, uninsured and underinsured claims can still matter.

Should I talk to the insurance company right away? Get guidance first whenever possible so you do not miss important details.
